# Free sources find Events. You.com supplies the evidence

---
status: accepted
---

The Scout checks free news RSS feeds, Google News RSS, and SEC EDGAR. It does not call You.com. A You.com search corroborates a new Event before research starts, and You.com Research builds the Dossier. Paying for news discovery would spend the monthly budget on checks that free feeds already cover.

## Consequences

- Thin coverage on a Watch Topic is a feed configuration problem, not a reason for the Scout to call You.com.
- Research spend is capped per Event, and a query log stops the same You.com query from being paid twice.
